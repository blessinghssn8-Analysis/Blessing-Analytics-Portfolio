Student Academic Performance Analysis

How do registration timing, engagement, and attendance relate to academic outcomes?

This case study summarizes previously undertaken work. Results and source materials still require verification before final publication.

Business question

How do registration timing, engagement, and attendance relate to academic outcomes?

Dataset and limitations

Academic dataset of 1,624 students; source and reuse permission to verify. Registration timing, VLE activity, attendance, coursework, exams, and final results.

Tools and methods

Excel, z-scores, descriptive statistics, ANOVA, multiple linear regression

Data preparation

Prepared standardized engagement indicators, a Keenness Index, registration groups, and analytical subsets.

Findings and visualization

Reported regression R² ≈ 0.270. Reported ANOVA F ≈ 32.71 and p ≈ 1.67 × 10⁻¹⁴. Reported group means: Early 62.09, Late 64.93, Normal 66.54. These figures need confirmation against the original workbook. Dashboard or notebook screenshot: not yet provided.

Recommendations

Do not use registration timing alone to identify at-risk students; examine engagement and attendance together.

What I would improve next

Observational associations are not causal. Reconcile group definitions and counts, test assumptions, and conduct post-hoc comparisons.
