Stock Market Data Analysis

What can historical opening and closing prices tell us, and what can they not predict?

This case study summarizes previously undertaken work. Results and source materials still require verification before final publication.

Business question

What can historical opening and closing prices tell us, and what can they not predict?

Dataset and limitations

Approximately 497,471 market records; source to verify. Symbol, date, open, close, high, low, volume.

Tools and methods

Python, pandas, exploratory analysis, simple linear regression

Data preparation

Inspected price fields and fitted a same-day close-versus-open model. Confirm any additional cleaning steps from the notebook.

Findings and visualization

Reported slope 0.9998745, intercept 0.0266775, R² 0.9997383, MSE 2.7261. The near-perfect fit is a same-day relationship, not evidence of future forecasting ability. Dashboard or notebook screenshot: not yet provided.

Recommendations

Analyze returns and volatility rather than interpreting this regression as a trading strategy.

What I would improve next

Use time-based validation, percentage returns, security-level comparisons and forward-looking targets.
